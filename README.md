# RideShare-Demo
 Students and staff share rides to save on fuel and traffic.
 <br>
 <br>
 ->Basic Functionality
1.Post a ride with your route, time, and available seats.
2.Search and book a seat on someone else's ride.
3.The driver marks the ride as started/finished.
4.Passengers rate the driver afterward.
<br>
->Tweaks
1.Filter rides by time or route.
2.See a driver's average rating before booking.
<br>
->Additional Features
1.In-app chat between driver and passengers to coordinate pickup details.
2.Recurring ride posting for people with the same daily commute.
3.A "favorite drivers/passengers" list for people you've ridden with before.
4.Cost-splitting calculator that suggests a fair fuel contribution per seat.
5.A passenger can be picked up from the middle of a route. (pickup checkpoint)
6.Drop Checkpoint. 
7.Cancellation policy with a visible cancellation count on each user's profile.
8.Admin view to monitor reported issues or safety concerns.
<br>
->Wow Factor:  
1.Female-Only Ride Option
2.Drivers can mark a ride as female-only.
3.Those rides are completely hidden from male users: not just greyed out, genuinely invisible to them.
