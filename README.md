# RideShare-Demo
 Students and staff share rides to save on fuel and traffic.
 <br>
 <br>
 <h3>->Basic Functionality</h3><br>
1. Post a ride with your route, time, and available seats.<br>
2. Search and book a seat on someone else's ride.<br>
3. The driver marks the ride as started/finished.<br>
4. Passengers rate the driver afterward.<br>
<br>
<h3>->Tweaks</h3><br>
1. Filter rides by time or route.<br>
2. See a driver's average rating before booking.<br>
<br>
<h3>->Additional Features</h3><br>
1. In-app chat between driver and passengers to coordinate pickup details.<br>
2. Recurring ride posting for people with the same daily commute.<br>
3. A "favorite drivers/passengers" list for people you've ridden with before.<br>
4. Cost-splitting calculator that suggests a fair fuel contribution per seat.<br>
5. A passenger can be picked up from the middle of a route. (pickup checkpoint)<br>
6. Drop Checkpoint. <br>
7. Cancellation policy with a visible cancellation count on each user's profile.<br>
8. Admin view to monitor reported issues or safety concerns.<br>
<br>
<h3>->Wow Factor:</h3>  <br>
1. Female-Only Ride Option<br>
2. Drivers can mark a ride as female-only.<br>
3. Those rides are completely hidden from male users: not just greyed out, genuinely invisible to them.<br>
